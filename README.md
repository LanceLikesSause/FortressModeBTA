# Fortress Mode

## Harder vanilla monsters

All Hostile Mobs: their speed is now as fast as the player's walking speed 
and their knockback is now half of the default.

Zombies: their HP is now 40 instead of  the usual 20 and Armored Zombies will now have 80 instead of 40.

Skeletons: will fire arrow quicker and their arrows are now faster, 
additionally they will back away from its target or the player.

Creepers: will jump towards the player when it's charged up.

Spiders: will shoot spiderwebs at the player or target.

Slimes: baby slimes will now deal damage.
